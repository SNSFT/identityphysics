Abstract

The electroweak sector has four bosons, and PNBA has four phases. This article places each boson in its phase using only measured masses and tree-level electroweak relations. The photon is massless with zero coupling, τ = 0: Noble. The W’s torsion is its weak coupling over its mass at electroweak capacity, τ = MW/(πv) = 0.1039: Locked. The Higgs torsion is its quartic coupling, λ = mH²/(2v²) = 0.1293: IVA. The Z’s torsion is the mixing angle read as torsion, τ = tan²θW = (MZ² − MW²)/MW² = 0.2874, 2.1 times TL: Shatter. Every assignment is settled by bounded uncertainty. The W holds Locked across MW ± σ, the Higgs holds IVA across mH ± σ, and the Z holds Shatter across both the W and Z mass bands. The ordering τγ < τW < TLIVA < τH < TL ≤ τZ follows. The Shatter boson also decays faster: ΓZ = 2.4955 GeV exceeds ΓW = 2.085 GeV beyond both uncertainties.
