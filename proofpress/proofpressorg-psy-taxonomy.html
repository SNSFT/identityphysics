Abstract

This article closes the PSY taxonomy series [9,9,2,51–54] by proving its findings together in one master theorem. Every identity state with P > 0 and B ≥ 0 falls into exactly one of three base phases: Noble (B = 0), Locked (0 < τ < TL) or Shatter (τ ≥ TL). Two structural dimensions refine each phase. Narrative status is intact when N ≥ 0.15 and void below it. Adaptation status is capable when A > 1.0 and flat otherwise. Together they give a 2 × 2 subtype matrix. The τ axis is divided into zones, with the IVA zone between TLIVA = 0.88·TL and TL. The resulting diagnostic order is τ for the zone, N for quality within the zone, A for capacity, Identity Mass as a secondary check, and the arrival route for how the state was reached. The states and counts come from Identity Collider runs; the theorems verify the taxonomy’s structure and each worked state.

What This Article Proves

  1. Completeness: every state with P > 0 and B ≥ 0 is Noble, Locked or Shatter, and every (N, A) pair falls in one of the four subtypes; the base phases are mutually exclusive (taxonomy_is_complete, noble_not_locked, locked_not_shatter, n_healthy_not_void).
  2. The thresholds are derived: TL = Ω₀/10 = 0.136899…, TLIVA = 0.88·TL = 0.120471… (TL_value, TL_IVA_value, TL_ratio, TL_IVA_ratio).
  3. F1, N-protection: the Shame-Internal reference state (N = 0.07) is Narrative-void and Shatter phase at τ = 0.20; any fusion with it takes the minimum N and falls below threshold; its self-fusion cancels to Noble (SI_N_void, SI_is_shatter_phase, n_bottleneck, n_protection_gradient).
  4. F2, sovereign IM invariance: three N+A+ reference states spanning Noble to the IVA zone have Identity Mass between 3.13 and 3.83, a maximum variation of about 22%, under 25%; IM alone does not register stress in sovereign identities (all_three_N_healthy, all_three_A_capable, im_invariance_across_zones).
  5. F3, DC zone splitting: DC-Sovereign (τ = 0.040) and DC-Resolution (τ ≈ 0.014) share the same τ zone, but their Identity Masses (≈ 4.94 and ≈ 2.82) differ by more than 2.0, so IM and arrival route separate them (dc_sovereign_tau_in_zone, dc_resolution_tau_in_zone, dc_im_gap_exceeds_2, dc_splitting_finding).
  6. F4, Depleted IVA: a collider state with τ ≈ 0.1357 (in the IVA zone), N = 0.08 (void) and A = 1.30 (capable) is Locked but Narrative-depleted, detectable only by measuring τ and N together (depleted_iva_verified, depleted_iva_joint_detection).
