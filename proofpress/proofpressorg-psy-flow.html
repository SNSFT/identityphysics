Abstract

Csikszentmihalyi’s channel model places experience on two axes, challenge and skill. Flow appears when both are high and matched; anxiety when challenge outruns skill; boredom when skill outruns challenge; apathy when both are low. This article reduces the model to PNBA. Skill is Pattern, challenge load is Behavior, feedback and calibration are Adaptation, and the sense of time is Narrative. The challenge-to-skill relation is read through torsion τ = B/P. Flow and the optimal channel are phase locked with Adaptation above 1. Anxiety is Shatter phase. Boredom and apathy are phase locked but under-engaged. The file’s new result concerns Narrative. In flow, Narrative drops below its floor as the narrative self goes offline (transient hypofrontality, Dietrich 2004) and time seems to disappear. That has the same low-N signature as the avoidant false lock in the attachment reduction, but the cause is the opposite: Narrative released by engagement with Adaptation high, rather than starved by rejection. The predicate flow_suppression separates the two.

What This Article Proves

  1. Flow suppression is never Shatter: a state with τ < TL, N ≤ 0.08 and A > 1 cannot be in the Shatter phase (flow_suppression_not_shatter, phase_lock_excludes_shatter).
  2. Challenge enters as Fext on Behavior only; enough of it drives Shatter (f_ext_preserves_pna, excess_challenge_causes_shatter, challenge_skill_is_torsion).
  3. Flow (P 1.0, N 0.05, B 0.12, A 1.1): τ = 0.12, phase locked, with flow suppression (flow_is_phase_locked, flow_has_suppression).
  4. Anxiety (P 0.4, N 1.2, B 0.22, A 0.5): τ = 0.55, Shatter (anxiety_is_shatter).
  5. Boredom (P 1.0, N 0.9, B 0.05, A 0.3) and apathy (P 0.2, N 0.3, B 0.02, A 0.15) are phase locked but are not flow: boredom keeps Narrative high, and apathy lacks the Adaptation (boredom_is_phase_locked, boredom_not_flow_suppression, apathy_is_phase_locked, apathy_not_flow_suppression).
  6. Optimal channel (P 1.2, N 0.05, B 0.15, A 1.3): τ = 0.125, phase locked, in flow, with IVA dominance (optimal_is_phase_locked, optimal_is_flow_suppression, optimal_iva_dominance).
