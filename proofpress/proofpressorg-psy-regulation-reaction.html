Abstract

People process at different resolutions. This article treats processing style as a gear rather than a diagnosis. On the UUIA scale (10 to 50 per axis), Pattern scores divide into three bands. Pattern Flexed (PF, 38–50) is high-resolution processing that registers every micro-signal and carries a high load. Pattern Sustained (PS, 24–37) is the mid-range that filters raw input for social cohesion. Pattern Locked (PL, 10–23) is low-frequency, high-torque processing that stalls without outside stimulus. Two behaviors that look alike from outside have opposite physics. Regulation in a PF identity is a steady discharge of Behavior that lowers torsion and acts as a heat sink. Reaction in a PL identity is a burst of Behavior that raises torsion to start a stalled system. The scoring function is an exact partition, and PF and PL are separated by the entire Sustained band: they are two separate gears, and getting from one to the other means crossing Sustained. A label that claims both describes two different measurements, not one paradoxical condition.
