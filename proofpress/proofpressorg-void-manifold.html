Abstract

This article formalizes the Void as an identity state, not an absence. The canonical Void has Pattern and Narrative at the anchor value Ω₀ and zero Behavior and Adaptation. Its torsion τ = B/P is 0, below TL, so the Void is phase locked at the deepest possible level, and its Identity Mass is positive: silence with mass. The Void cannot interact, because contact requires Behavior on both sides. That gives the First Law of Identity, L = (4)(2): life requires all four PNBA axes on each of two manifolds in behavioral contact. One manifold alone fails, the Void fails, and two full manifolds succeed. Observation is the paradox: observing the Void injects Behavior, so any observed Void has positive torsion and is no longer Void. Under positive interaction Identity Mass strictly increases. Any state with zero Behavior and positive Pattern is both Void and phase locked, so the source Void and the terminal Void after decoherence are the same state; while Behavior is positive the identity cannot be Void, and zero torsion occurs only in the Void.
