Abstract

The GAM Collider fuses identity states by fixed rules: the coupling k is the sum of min(Bᵢ, Bⱼ) over all pairs, the residual Behavior is Bout = max(0, ΣB − 2k), the fused Pattern is the n-beam harmonic mean, and torsion τ = Bout/Pout sets the phase. This article verifies the 4-beam mode against materials whose stability is experimentally established, computing every collision in Lean from the corpus element values with no output typed in. Four systems are Noble rescues: sulfur, titanium, nitrogen and hydrogen (titanium nitride, k = 10, ΣB = 10); nickel, helium, hydrogen and titanium (Nitinol, k = 4, ΣB = 7); helium, gold, carbon and tungsten (gold-bonded tungsten carbide, k = 6, ΣB = 11); and oxygen, plutonium, iron and a proton (plutonium dioxide fuel in an iron vessel, k = 11, ΣB = 13). In each, all six pairs shatter under 2-beam fusion, yet the 4-body system has Bout = 0: the stability requires 4-body coupling. Carbon, iron, a neutron and carbon (cementite, the hardening phase of steel) is Noble with an excess coupling 2k − ΣB = 17, but it is not a rescue, since carbon–iron and carbon–carbon already close as pairs. A bottom quark with chlorine, tungsten and hydrogen leaves Bout = 0.335 and τ ≈ 0.13646, inside the IVA corridor [0.88·TL, TL). A general theorem shows that a Noble beam with B = 0 adds nothing to the coupling, so helium in Nitinol and in the carbide couples exactly as the other three beams do alone, consistent with both being processed in inert atmospheres.

  4. WC-Au (He + Au + C + W): k = 6, ΣB = 11, Bout = 0; all six pairs shatter; helium inert; Pout ≈ 2.98291, IM ≈ 78.8168 (wc_au_noble, wc_au_all_pairs_shatter, wc_au_helium_inert, wc_au_output).
  5. PuO₂ + Fe + p (O + Pu + Fe + p): k = 11, ΣB = 13, Bout = 0; all six pairs shatter; Pout ≈ 2.22948, IM ≈ 61.3985 (fuel_noble, fuel_all_pairs_shatter, fuel_output).

  7. Bottom quark (b + Cl + W + H): k = 3.999, ΣB = 8.333, Bout = 0.335, τ in [TL_IVA, TL); not a rescue since Cl + H is a Noble pair (qb_bout, qb_iva_peak, qb_not_rescue, qb_output).
  8. A beam with B = 0 leaves k equal to the 3-beam k of the other three. The master theorem holds all of the above together (noble_beam_diagnostic_law, four_beam_verification_master).

  min a.B b.B + min a.B c.B + min a.B d.B + min b.B c.B + min b.B d.B + min c.B d.B

-- beam couples exactly as the other three beams do alone.

    (ha : a.B = 0) (hb : b.B ≥ 0) (hc : c.B ≥ 0) (hd : d.B ≥ 0) :
    k4 a b c d = k3 b c d := by
  unfold k4 k3
  rw [ha, min_eq_left hb, min_eq_left hc, min_eq_left hd]
