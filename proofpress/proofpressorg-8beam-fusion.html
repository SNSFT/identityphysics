Abstract

The GAM Collider fuses n identity states by rules that do not change with the number of beams: the fused Pattern is the n-body harmonic mean n/Σ(1/Pᵢ), Narrative adds, Adaptation takes the maximum, and Behavior keeps the residual max(0, ΣB − 2k) after k bonds, with k at most the sum of min(Bᵢ, Bⱼ) over all pairs. This article proves the rules at n = 8, where the coupling runs over 28 pairs. The fused Pattern is positive and never exceeds eight times the smallest input, Narrative and Adaptation dominate every input, and adding bonds strictly lowers Behavior and torsion. A system is Noble exactly when its Behavior sum is at most twice the coupling. The 8-beam engine contains the smaller ones: with four beams passive the coupling is exactly the 4-beam coupling, and with six passive it is the 2-beam min(B₁, B₂). Eight equal-B beams always close Noble, using exactly 1/7 of their coupling capacity, and a beam with B = 0 contributes nothing to any pair. Computed from corpus element values, hydrogen–carbon–nitrogen–oxygen doubled (k = 50), water quadrupled (k = 34) and gallium arsenide quadrupled (k = 84) are all Noble. Doubling also opens Noble closure: tungsten, hydrogen and two helium beams leave Bout = 5 at four beams, while the same composition doubled closes Noble at eight.

What This Article Proves

  1. Fusion rules at n = 8: Pout > 0 and Pout ≤ 8·Pᵢ for every input; Nout and Aout dominate every input; more bonds lower B; kmax8 ≥ 0 (t1_p_fused8_positive, t2_p_fused8_capped, t3_n_fused8_adds, t4_b_decreases_with_bonds, t5_a_fused8_dominates, t6_k_max8_nonneg).
  2. Noble condition Bsum ≤ 2·kmax8 gives Bout = 0; Noble requires Bsum ≤ 2k (t7_noble_condition, t10_noble_implies_bond_parity).
  3. Doubling emergence: W + H + He + He has k = 1 and Bout = 5 at four beams; doubled, k = 11 and Bout = 0 (t8_doubling_emergence).
  4. Equal-B eight beams are always Noble, with kmax8 = 28·B and headroom 1/7 (t9_equal_b_eight_beam_noble, t21_k_max8_equal_b_scaling, t22_eight_beam_noble_headroom).
  5. Computed verifications: CHON×2 (k = 50), water×4 (k = 34), GaAs×4 (k = 84), all with Bout = 0 (t11_chon_double_noble, t12_water_octuple_noble, t13_gaas_8beam_noble).
  6. Inheritance: with e5–e8 at B = 0 the coupling is the 4-beam coupling; with e3–e8 at B = 0 it is min(B₁, B₂) and B follows the 2-beam formula (t15_four_beam_inheritance, t16_two_beam_inheritance, t17_b_two_beam_inheritance).
  7. More bonds strictly lower τ; phase lock holds exactly when the residual Behavior is below TL·Pout; a B = 0 beam contributes zero to all its pairs. The master theorem holds the rules together (t18_more_bonds_lower_tau, t19_stability_condition, t20_noble_no_contribution, eight_beam_fusion_master).

-- The fused P never exceeds eight times any single input: the beam with
-- the smallest P dominates the harmonic sum.
theorem harmonic_cap (x S : ℝ) (hx : 0 < x) (hS : 1/x ≤ S) : 8 / S ≤ 8 * x := by
  have hS0 : 0 < S := lt_of_lt_of_le (one_div_pos.mpr hx) hS
  rw [div_le_iff₀ hS0]
  have h := mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left hS (le_of_lt hx)
  rw [mul_one_div_cancel (ne_of_gt hx)] at h
  nlinarith
