## 11. Frame independence

## 16. Misappropriation-Risk Clarification (MRC)

### 16.1 What is established

The results in this paper are real and testable. Every placement is arithmetic on published
quantities, carried out in exact rational arithmetic, and every one of them can be checked by anyone
with the published figure and a calculator. Specifically:

**The two readings and the bridge are exact.** Δv = c · τ_A · τ_B follows from the Tsiolkovsky
equation by substitution. Anyone can verify it in a line.

**The placements are arithmetic on published specifications.** The eighteen engine placements, the
eight vehicle placements, the forty-two-nuclide census and the seven mission rows each reduce to a
division or a logarithm against a published number. They are checkable, and they are stated to the
precision the inputs support.

**The corridor edges carry no fitted parameter.** Light speed, the fine-structure constant and the
integer 1001 fix them. A reader who disagrees with an edge must disagree with one of those three.

**The conversion fraction is a published quantity.** It is Aston's packing fraction, and the
identification is exact. The census reproduces published values, including the packing fraction of
iron-56 at −11.62.

These are results about **composition and placement, not about process.** That distinction is the
whole of this section, and if a reader takes only one sentence from this paper, that is the one to
take.

### 16.2 What is a different question

This paper places drives, vehicles, reactions and nuclides on a torsion axis. It does not describe how
to build, operate, fuel, contain, cool, shield, trigger or test any of them, and it contains no
construction information, no process information, no operating parameters and no device design.

**Placement is not permission, and a number is not a procedure.** That a reaction's conversion
fraction places inside the corridor says where it sits on one axis. It says nothing about whether the
reaction can be initiated, sustained, contained, directed, or made to do work — and every one of those
is a separate question answered by laboratory and engineering practice, not by arithmetic on mass
defects.

**The energy sources named in Sections 8 and 9 are named because their conversion fractions are
published.** Fission, fusion, the stellar chain and annihilation appear as entries on a ladder. The
materials in the census include radioactive nuclides — uranium-235, uranium-238 and thorium-232 among
them — and they appear there as published masses. Nothing in this paper bears on obtaining, handling,
processing, enriching, or using any of them.

**An exact placement is not a reduced hazard.** The exactness is why every remaining variable is
process. A composition stated precisely tells a reader where it sits; it does not tell them that the
conditions required to realize it are within reach, safe, legal, or achievable with the equipment in
front of them.

### 16.3 Where the risk actually lives

The risk in this domain belongs to **laboratory conditions and environmental F_ext** — the external
forcing a real bench applies that the arithmetic does not model. It does not belong to the
mathematics, and no sentence in this paper should be read as doubt about a result. The placements hold.
What varies is the process, and process is where people get hurt.

**Hazard scales with which controls are present and with scale, not with the venue.** The relevant
questions are whether there is containment rated for the energy density in play, whether there is
shielding appropriate to the radiation, whether there is interlocking that fails safe, whether there
is monitoring that reads out in real time, whether there is a trained second person present, and
whether the quantity has been kept at the smallest scale that answers the question. A well-equipped
independent facility that has those controls is not the concern. The underfunded shortcut is — the
setup that reads a precise number, infers that precision removes the need for a control, and proceeds
at a scale that leaves no margin.

**The controls are the variable, not the building.**

### 16.4 Handling practice

For anyone working near the material this paper places:

**Radioactive nuclides.** The census includes naturally radioactive species. Possession, transport and
use are regulated, and the regulation is the operative constraint regardless of what any placement
says. Work with licensed material under the license, with the dosimetry, shielding and survey practice
the license requires, and with the institutional or jurisdictional radiation safety authority engaged
before material is on a bench.

**High energy density generally.** Stored energy injures by release, not by magnitude on paper.
Pressure vessels, cryogens, high voltage, pulsed power, and plasma all have established practice, and
the practice exists because the failure modes are known. Follow the practice for the specific hazard
group in play, at the scale in play.

**Scale discipline.** Answer the question at the smallest scale that answers it. Precision in a
composition is a reason to reduce scale, not to increase it — a known target means the experiment can
be small.

**Second-person presence.** Work that can injure is not solo work. The control that most reliably
prevents a bad outcome from becoming a worse one is another trained person who can act.

**When the hazard group changes, update this section.** Any work that extends this reduction to
materials or reactions not placed here carries the hazard groups of those materials, and the handling
practice must be restated for them specifically before the work begins. A placement added without its
hazard group is an incomplete placement.

### 16.5 What this section is for

Parents, teachers, reviewers and program managers make real decisions from papers like this one. A
paper that reads as unreal gets no respect for its safety content, and a reader who concludes the
results are hypothetical will not extend the care the materials require. So: the results are real, the
arithmetic is checkable, the placements hold — and the conditions under which anyone would act on them
are governed by practice this paper does not supply and does not replace.
