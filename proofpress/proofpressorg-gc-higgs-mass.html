Abstract

The Standard Model relates the Higgs mass to its quartic self-coupling, mH² = 2λv², and takes λ as a free parameter. With the PDG 2024 mass mH = 125.20 ± 0.11 GeV and v = 246.22 GeV, λ = 0.12928. In PNBA this coupling is the Higgs torsion, and it sits inside the IVA corridor (0.12047, 0.13690), 0.536 of the way across. The assignment is settled by bounded uncertainty: for every mass in the 1σ band the coupling stays in IVA, and the same holds across ±30σ, 121.9 to 128.5 GeV. The nearest corridor edge is about 33σ from the measured mass, so no refinement of the measurement within its error budget can change the phase. Read the other way, the corridor and the VEV alone, with no Higgs data, confine any IVA coupling to a mass between 120.8 and 128.9 GeV. The measured Higgs lies inside that 8 GeV window. The Higgs gives mass to other particles by coupling them, and the mass-giving catalyst occupies the transition zone between Locked and Shatter itself.

  if τ = 0 then Phase.Noble
  else if τ < TL_IVA then Phase.Locked
  else if τ < TORSION_LIMIT then Phase.IVA
  else Phase.Shatter

  have h1 := mul_self_le_mul_self h0 hlo
  have h2 := mul_self_le_mul_self (le_trans h0 hlo) hhi
  unfold lambda_of
  constructor
  · rw [lt_div_iff₀ hv]; nlinarith
  · rw [div_lt_iff₀ hv]; nlinarith
