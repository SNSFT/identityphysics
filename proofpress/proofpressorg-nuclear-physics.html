Abstract

The binding energy per nucleon, B/A, is the central measured quantity of nuclear structure. It rises steeply from deuterium (1.112 MeV) through the doubly magic helium-4 (7.074 MeV), peaks in the iron-nickel group near 8.8 MeV, and falls slowly to 7.570 MeV at uranium-238. This article maps a nucleus to PNBA by taking B as B/A divided by the proton rest energy 938.272 MeV, P as the anchor-native scale Pbase = (Ω₀/fH)1/3 ≈ 0.988, N as the mass number and A as the decay constant. A free nucleon has no binding and is Noble. Every bound nucleus is Locked: τ lies between 0.0012 for deuterium and 0.0095 at the peak, and any binding per nucleon up to the 16 MeV volume term of saturated nuclear matter stays below TLIVA. The ordering of the measured values places the top of the curve at Ni-62, above Fe-58 and Fe-56, and the energy released by fusion below the peak or fission above it is Q = A·ΔB/A: 23.848 MeV for D + D → He-4 and more than 190 MeV for U-238 splitting into fragments near 8.4 MeV per nucleon. The Bethe–Weizsäcker formula with Krane's coefficients, evaluated with proved bounds on the cube root of 56, gives Fe-56 a binding per nucleon between 8.77 and 8.79 MeV against the measured 8.790. He-4 and O-16 are local binding peaks against all their neighbors, the signature of shell closure. The couplings that build nuclei are Shatter: the pion–nucleon coupling g²/4π ≈ 14 and the strong coupling αs ≈ 0.31 at the τ-lepton scale. As αs runs to 0.118 at MZ it falls below TLIVA, so asymptotic freedom carries the strong coupling across the torsion limit. The neutron–proton mass difference is Locked, free-neutron β-decay releases 0.782 MeV, and the Chandrasekhar mass of an iron core, 1.258 M☉, lies below that of carbon-oxygen matter, 1.459 M☉.

-- Cube roots: (r^(1/3))³ = r, so cube-root bounds follow from bounds on r.
theorem cube_rpow (r : ℝ) (hr : 0 ≤ r) : (r ^ ((1:ℝ)/3)) ^ 3 = r := by
  rw [← Real.rpow_natCast, ← Real.rpow_mul hr]; norm_num

theorem cbrt_gt (r c : ℝ) (hr : 0 ≤ r) (h : c ^ 3 < r) : r ^ ((1:ℝ)/3) > c := by
  by_contra hle
  push Not at hle
  have hy : 0 ≤ r ^ ((1:ℝ)/3) := Real.rpow_nonneg hr _
  have h3 := pow_le_pow_left₀ hy hle 3
  rw [cube_rpow r hr] at h3
  linarith

theorem cbrt_lt (r c : ℝ) (hr : 0 ≤ r) (hc : 0 ≤ c) (h : r < c ^ 3) :
    r ^ ((1:ℝ)/3) < c := by
  by_contra hle
  push Not at hle
  have h3 := pow_le_pow_left₀ hc hle 3
  rw [cube_rpow r hr] at h3
  linarith
