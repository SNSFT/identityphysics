Abstract

The companion article on functional emotions asks what emotions are structurally. This article asks how emotional state is sampled from a living identity. The APPA Emotional Primitives instrument reads ten named signals, each scored from four items on a 1–5 scale, for a range of 4 to 20: a score of 9 or below labels the signal low, 10 to 14 mid, and 15 or above high. Each signal, when dominant, is reduced to a PNBA state with a torsion value and a phase. The ten signals fall into four structural clusters. Threat (τ = 0.20), Overwhelm (τ = 0.60) and Anger (τ = 0.20) are Shatter. Loss (N = 0.08) and Shame (N = 0.06) are false locks: torsion passes, Narrative is depleted. Desire, Pride, Connection and Safety are true locks, with Safety holding the floor of the cluster at τ = 0.09. Play, Adaptation above 1 with Pattern expanded, is the one signal at IVA peak. Across the positive signals and the Narrative-depleting negative signals, valence is the side of the Narrative threshold a signal sits on.
