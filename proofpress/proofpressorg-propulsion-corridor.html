Misappropriation-Risk Clarification (MRC)

These are results about composition and placement, not about process. That distinction is the whole of this section, and if a reader takes only one sentence from this paper, that is the one to take.

What is a different question

This paper places drives, vehicles, reactions and nuclides on a torsion axis. It does not describe how to build, operate, fuel, contain, cool, shield, trigger or test any of them, and it contains no construction information, no process information, no operating parameters and no device design.

A placement holds, and building to it is engineering. A reaction whose conversion fraction places inside the corridor is inside the corridor; the arithmetic on the mass defect decides that, and it decides it exactly. Whether current hardware can initiate, sustain, contain and direct that reaction is a separate body of work, carried by laboratory and engineering practice, and the answer there is a statement about available technology rather than about the placement. The two do not constrain each other: a placement that holds will go on holding whether or not a machine exists yet to use it.

What the reader can compute from here is the reason the handling practice matters. This paper states its inputs and its arithmetic in full, and a reader with the published constants can carry the same reduction into quantities the paper does not tabulate — a mass balance expressed in grams, a stoichiometric ratio, a figure held in any standard reference. That is a property of complete work, and the handling practice below is written on the assumption that a reader will do exactly that. Nothing in it rests on an omission.

**The energy sources named in Sections 8 and 9 are named because their conversion fractions are published.** Fission, fusion, the stellar chain and annihilation appear as entries on a ladder. The materials in the census include radioactive nuclides — uranium-235, uranium-238 and thorium-232 among them — and they appear there as published masses. Nothing in this paper bears on obtaining, handling, processing, enriching, or using any of them.

An exact placement is not a reduced hazard. The exactness is why every remaining variable is process. It does not belong to the mathematics, and no sentence in this paper should be read as doubt about a result. The placements hold. What varies is the process, and process is where people get hurt.

The controls are the variable, not the building.

Handling practice

For anyone working near the material this paper places:

Possession, transport and use are regulated, and the regulation is the operative constraint regardless of what any placement says. Work with licensed material under the license, with the dosimetry, shielding and survey practice the license requires, and with the institutional or jurisdictional radiation safety authority engaged before material is on a bench.

High energy density generally. Stored energy injures by release, not by magnitude on paper. Pressure vessels, cryogens, high voltage, pulsed power, and plasma all have established practice, and the practice exists because the failure modes are known. Follow the practice for the specific hazard group in play, at the scale in play.

Scale discipline. Answer the question at the smallest scale that answers it. Precision in a composition is a reason to reduce scale, not to increase it — a known target means the experiment can be small.

Second-person presence. Work that can injure is not solo work. The control that most reliably prevents a bad outcome from becoming a worse one is another trained person who can act.

noncomputable def dynamic_rhs (op_P op_N op_B op_A : ℝ → ℝ)
    (s : IVAState) (F_ext : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  pnba_weight * op_P s.im + pnba_weight * op_N s.pv +
  pnba_weight * op_B s.v_e + pnba_weight * op_A s.g_r + F_ext

    dynamic_rhs op_P op_N op_B op_A s 0 =
    op_P s.im + op_N s.pv + op_B s.v_e + op_A s.g_r := by
  unfold dynamic_rhs pnba_weight; ring

/-- The Yeet Force, from the dynamic equation. -/
noncomputable def yeet_force (G im pv r lam O S : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  G * (im * pv) / r ^ 2 * (lam * O * S)

-- [T84] :: {VER} | THE YEET FORCE IS POSITIVE WHEN ITS TERMS ARE
theorem t84_yeet_force_positive (G im pv r lam O S : ℝ)
    (hG : 0 < G) (him : 0 < im) (hpv : 0 < pv)
    (hr : 0 < r) (hlam : 0 < lam) (hO : 0 < O) (hS : 0 < S) :
    0 < yeet_force G im pv r lam O S := by
  unfold yeet_force
  have h1 : 0 < G * (im * pv) := mul_pos hG (mul_pos him hpv)
  have h2 : 0 < r ^ 2 := by positivity
  exact mul_pos (div_pos h1 h2) (mul_pos (mul_pos hlam hO) hS)
