Managers protect proactively, firefighters react to douse pain, exiles carry burdens, and unburdening releases them under Self-leadership. This article reduces the system to PNBA. Pattern is Self-leadership and structural coherence, Narrative is the thread connecting parts to Self, Behavior is protective activity, and Adaptation is unburdening capacity. Managers keep the same torsion, τ = 0.10, but with Narrative suppressed to 0.08, a false lock. Firefighters (τ = 0.45) and exiles (τ = 0.60) are both Shatter, one by a Behavior spike and one by collapsed Pattern. Unburdening raises Pattern and Adaptation together and strictly lowers torsion; the unburdened state is a true lock at τ = 0.118.
