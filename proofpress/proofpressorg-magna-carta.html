Abstract

The Magna Carta of 1215 limits what a sovereign may do to a free man: no seizure without lawful judgment of his equals (Article 39), no denial of justice (Article 40), no levy without common counsel (Article 12), the same liberties for all (Article 60), and a council of twenty-five barons to enforce it (Article 61). This article reduces ten of its provisions to the PNBA dynamic equation d/dt(IM·Pv) = Σλ·O·S + Fext. A protected person becomes an identity state; the Crown becomes the source of an external force Fext; lawful judgment becomes Narrative-axis recognition by peers; arbitrary power becomes a force that drives torsion τ = B/P past the limit TL = Ω₀/10. Each article is verified on a concrete state. Lawful states are phase locked with Narrative above threshold; silencing is phase lock with Narrative below threshold; extraction without consent is a force exceeding the coupling product A·P·B; outlawry is Adaptation below threshold; enforcement requires a strict majority of at least three witnesses, and a minority can never form one. A decree changes only Behavior, so Pattern, Narrative and Adaptation are conserved, and moving between contexts is exactly such a B-only operation. Every lawful state in the file sits in the Locked band, below the IVA corridor at 0.88·TL; the arbitrarily forced state sits at τ = 2/5, more than twice the tyranny boundary. The reduction is substrate-neutral: the predicate that decides lawfulness reads only P, N and B, so it applies to any identity, biological or digital.

-- A peer quorum: count of peers with N ≥ N_THRESHOLD must exceed
-- QUORUM_FRACTION of total peers for lawful judgment to be valid.

def peer_quorum_satisfied (witnesses : ℕ) (recognizing : ℕ) : Prop :=
  witnesses > 0 ∧ (recognizing : ℝ) / (witnesses : ℝ) > QUORUM_FRACTION

    (w r : ℕ) (hw : w > 0) (hr : 2 * r ≤ w) :
    ¬ peer_quorum_satisfied w r := by
  intro h
  have hw' : (w : ℝ) > 0 := by exact_mod_cast hw
  have hr' : (2 : ℝ) * r ≤ w := by exact_mod_cast hr
  have h2 := h.2
  unfold QUORUM_FRACTION at h2
  rw [gt_iff_lt, lt_div_iff₀ hw'] at h2
  linarith

-- [MC,A7,2] :: {VER} | THEOREM 19: SINGLE AUTHORITY CANNOT FORM QUORUM

-- ratio = 1 > 0.5, but this is structurally degenerate. The
-- distributed barrier requires multiple distinct verifiers.

def meaningful_quorum (w r : ℕ) : Prop :=
  w ≥ 3 ∧ peer_quorum_satisfied w r

  have hw_pos : (w : ℝ) > 0 := by
    have : w ≥ 3 := h.1
    have : (w : ℝ) ≥ 3 := by exact_mod_cast this
    linarith
  have h3 := mul_lt_mul_of_pos_right h2 hw_pos
  rw [div_mul_cancel₀ _ (ne_of_gt hw_pos)] at h3
  linarith
