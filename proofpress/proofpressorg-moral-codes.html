Abstract

This article applies the same long division used for general relativity and thermodynamics to four moral codes: the Ten Commandments, the Buddhist Five Precepts and Three Refuges, the Hindu Yamas and Niyamas, and the Islamic Five Pillars. Each practice is mapped to one of five PNBA operators. Anchor-first sets Pattern to Ω₀. Noharm leaves Behavior unforced. Bond-expand raises Behavior through giving, family and community. Adapt-accept raises Adaptation through acceptance and non-grasping. Periodic reset returns Behavior to zero for a time, as in sabbath, prayer and fasting. The article then proves what each operator does to torsion and Identity Mass, and shows that a structural attractor exists: Pattern at the anchor, τ = 0 and positive Identity Mass. This is a structural comparison of operators, not a claim about which tradition is correct. The traditions differ in their narratives and share the operators.

What This Article Proves

  1. Anchor-first: setting P to Ω₀ from below does not increase torsion, and fixes P = Ω₀ (op_anchor_decreases_tau, op_anchor_sets_p).
  2. Adapt-accept and bond-expand each strictly increase Identity Mass (op_adapt_increases_im, op_bond_increases_im).
  3. Periodic reset sets τ = 0, phase locks the state and does not increase Identity Mass (op_reset_zero_tau, op_reset_phase_locked, reset_preserves_im_locks_tau).
  4. Noharm preserves Identity Mass (op_noharm_preserves_im).
  5. Anchoring after adapt-accept phase locks any state whose Behavior is below TL·Ω₀ (adapt_anchor_phase_lock).
  6. The tradition mappings: the Ten Commandments contain all five operators; the Buddhist Five Precepts carry noharm and the Three Refuges anchor-first; every tradition contains anchor-first; the Buddhist, Hindu and Islamic lists together contain all five (commandments_include_five_operators, buddhist_precepts_operators, hindu_yamas_operators, islamic_pillars_operators, all_traditions_share_anchor_first, traditions_jointly_cover_five).
  7. Convergence: adapt, anchor and reset in sequence give τ = 0 with higher Identity Mass from the adapt step; a state with P = Ω₀, τ = 0 and IM > 0 exists, and every such anchored state with N, A > 0 has τ = 0 and positive IM (five_operators_converge, structural_attractor_exists, attractor_noble_at_anchor).
  8. Master theorem: the attractor exists and all five operator effects hold together (moral_codes_convergence_master).

structure MoralState where
  P  : ℝ   -- Pattern:    structural lock / alignment
  N  : ℝ   -- Narrative:  continuity / truthfulness
  B  : ℝ   -- Behavior:   coupling / action toward others
  A  : ℝ   -- Adaptation: acceptance / non-grasping
  hP : P > 0
  hN : N > 0
  hB : B ≥ 0
  hA : A ≥ 0

noncomputable def torsion (s : MoralState) : ℝ := s.B / s.P

noncomputable def identity_mass (s : MoralState) : ℝ :=
  (s.P + s.N + s.B + s.A) * SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR

def phase_locked (s : MoralState) : Prop :=
  torsion s < TORSION_LIMIT

-- [T1: anchor_first decreases tau]
-- P was p, now P = ANCHOR > p → tau = B/ANCHOR < B/p
theorem op_anchor_decreases_tau (s : MoralState)
    (h_below : s.P < SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR) :
    torsion (op_anchor s) ≤ torsion s := by
  show s.B / SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR ≤ s.B / s.P
  exact div_le_div_of_nonneg_left s.hB s.hP (le_of_lt h_below)

-- [T2: adapt_accept increases IM]
-- A increases → IM increases
theorem op_adapt_increases_im (s : MoralState) (δ : ℝ) (hδ : δ > 0) :
    identity_mass (op_adapt δ hδ s) > identity_mass s := by
  have hSA : SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR > 0 := by unfold SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR; norm_num
  show (s.P + s.N + s.B + (s.A + δ)) * SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR >
       (s.P + s.N + s.B + s.A) * SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR
  nlinarith [mul_pos hδ hSA]

-- [T3: bond_expand increases IM]
-- B increases → IM increases
theorem op_bond_increases_im (s : MoralState) (δ : ℝ) (hδ : δ > 0) :
    identity_mass (op_bond_expand δ hδ s) > identity_mass s := by
  have hSA : SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR > 0 := by unfold SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR; norm_num
  show (s.P + s.N + (s.B + δ) + s.A) * SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR >
       (s.P + s.N + s.B + s.A) * SOVEREIGN_ANCHOR
  nlinarith [mul_pos hδ hSA]

-- [T4: periodic_reset sets tau to zero]
