Abstract

Rotter’s locus of control describes whether a person believes outcomes come from their own actions (internal) or from luck, fate and powerful others (external). Internal locus is associated with better performance, health and wellbeing; external locus with higher rates of depression and poorer coping. Seligman’s learned helplessness is the extreme case: after repeated uncontrollable events, an organism stops trying even when escape becomes possible. This article reduces the construct to PNBA. Perceived control is Pattern, the explanatory story is Narrative, effort output is Behavior, and learning from outcomes is Adaptation. External forces enter as Fext. Internal locus is phase locked, with IVA dominance at the strong end. External locus is Shatter phase, with control eroded and action reactive. Learned helplessness is a distinct regime within Shatter: Pattern collapses and Adaptation drops below its floor (A < 0.15), so the identity stops updating. This is why active intervention, which restarts the Adaptation axis, is needed to reverse it.

What This Article Proves

  1. Learned helplessness is a strict sub-case of Shatter, marked by Adaptation below its floor (helplessness_implies_shatter, helplessness_a_dropout, phase_lock_excludes_shatter).
  2. Uncontrollable Fext changes Behavior only, and enough of it pushes the identity into Shatter, the first step on the helplessness pathway (f_ext_preserves_pna, repeated_fext_causes_shatter).
  3. Strong internal (P 1.0, N 0.9, B 0.10, A 1.1): τ = 0.10, phase locked, with IVA dominance (strong_internal_phase_locked, strong_internal_iva).
  4. Moderate internal (P 0.7, N 0.7, B 0.08, A 0.85): τ = 0.114, phase locked (moderate_internal_phase_locked).
  5. Moderate external (P 0.4, B 0.18): τ = 0.45, Shatter; strong external (P 0.25, B 0.15, A 0.3): τ = 0.60, Shatter but not helpless (moderate_external_is_shatter, strong_external_is_shatter, strong_external_not_helpless).
  6. Learned helplessness (P 0.10, N 0.3, B 0.02, A 0.08): τ = 0.20, Shatter with Adaptation dropout (learned_helpless_is_shatter, learned_helpless_a_dropout).
  7. Helplessness is worse than strong external locus: both are Shatter, but only the first has lost Adaptation (helpless_worse_than_external).
  8. One locus step is one step of the dynamic equation; all examples pass Step 6, and a master theorem fires them with the interlock (locus_step_is_dynamic_step, sovereign_lossy_exclusive, locus_all_examples_lossless, locus_is_lossless_pnba_projection).
