Abstract

Lin et al. (Nature 658, 365–371, 2026) report the first experimental observation of critical topology: topological boundary modes that persist at gapless critical points, where the bulk energy gap closes. Using engineered phononic crystals, they measure the entanglement spectrum in one and two dimensions, find a pair of mid-gap entanglement modes at exactly 0.5 in nontrivial cases, and observe multi-critical points where boundaries with distinct critical topology meet. This article reduces those results to PNBA. In the two-coupling chain the experiment tests, the intercell coupling is Behavior and the intracell coupling is Pattern, so τ = B/P = w/v. The gap closes exactly at τ = 1: the critical point is the B = P balance point, at 1/TL ≈ 7.3 × TL. In the general chain every zero of the model function is a coupling channel with τ = 1/r. A zero inside the unit circle is a channel past balance and contributes an edge mode; a zero on the circle is a channel at balance and closes the gap. Critical topology is one channel at balance while another is past it, and a multi-critical point is two channels at balance together. The 0.5 entanglement mode is the same balance across the entanglement cut, where τ = (1 − ξ)/ξ equals 1 exactly at ξ = ½, carrying the maximal single-mode entropy ln 2. Because τ depends only on coupling ratios, the classical acoustic and quantum platforms share every assignment.

What This Article Proves

  1. The SSH gap closes exactly at τ = w/v = 1, the B = P balance point; τ < 1 is the trivial phase and τ > 1 the topological phase (t1_gap_closes_iff_balance, t2_trivial_below_balance, t3_topological_past_balance).
  2. The zero of the chain lies inside the unit circle exactly when τ > 1, and τ = 1/r (t4_zero_inside_iff_past_balance).
  3. The fully dimerized chain (w = 0) is Noble: τ = 0, with the full intracell gap 2v (t5_dimer_limit_noble).
  4. The balance point lies between 7 TL and 8 TL (t6_balance_point_vs_TL).
  5. Substrate neutrality: τ is invariant when every coupling is rescaled, and the gap scales linearly (t7_scale_invariance).
  6. Every zero is a coupling channel: inside the unit circle means τ > 1, on the circle means τ = 1 (t8_inside_iff_past_balance, t9_on_circle_iff_balance).
  7. Instances of every case: gapped phases with 0, 1 and 2 edge modes; a trivial critical point; a nontrivial critical point with one protected edge mode; a multi-critical point (t10_gapped_phases through t13_multicritical_instance).
  8. Critical topology is one channel at τ = 1 with another at τ > 1; multi-criticality is two channels at τ = 1 (t14_critical_topology_in_tau, t15_multicritical_in_tau).
  9. The entanglement mid-gap mode ξ = ½ is exactly τcut = 1; it carries entropy ln 2; the spectrum is symmetric about ½ (t16_midgap_is_balance, t17_midgap_entropy, t18_entropy_symmetric).
  10. A master theorem fires the full reduction (critical_topology_is_balance).

noncomputable def tau_cell (v w : ℝ) : ℝ := w / v

-- [T1] :: {VER} | THE GAP CLOSES EXACTLY AT τ = 1 (B = P)
theorem t1_gap_closes_iff_balance (v w : ℝ) (hv : 0 < v) :
    ssh_gap v w = 0 ↔ tau_cell v w = 1 := by
  unfold ssh_gap tau_cell
  rw [div_eq_one_iff_eq hv.ne']
  constructor
  · intro h
    have h1 : |v - w| = 0 := by linarith
    have h2 : v - w = 0 := abs_eq_zero.mp h1
    linarith
  · intro h
    rw [h, sub_self, abs_zero, mul_zero]

noncomputable def edge_modes (ra rb : ℝ) : ℕ :=
  (if ra < 1 then 1 else 0) + (if rb < 1 then 1 else 0)

def is_critical (ra rb : ℝ) : Prop := ra = 1 ∨ rb = 1

def critical_topological (ra rb : ℝ) : Prop :=
  (ra = 1 ∧ rb < 1) ∨ (rb = 1 ∧ ra < 1)

def multicritical (ra rb : ℝ) : Prop := ra = 1 ∧ rb = 1

  rw [div_eq_one_iff_eq hr.ne']

noncomputable def es_entropy (ξ : ℝ) : ℝ :=

  · intro h; linarith
