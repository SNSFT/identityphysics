Abstract

Dweck’s work on implicit theories of ability (Mueller and Dweck, 1998; Dweck, 2006) distinguishes a fixed mindset, in which ability is set, from a growth mindset, in which it develops through effort. This article reduces the distinction to the Adaptation axis. Pattern is current competence, Narrative is the identity story around ability, Behavior is the response to challenge, and Adaptation is the capacity to expand Pattern. Fixed mindset is Adaptation dropout in every form: under threat (A = 0.10) the challenge spikes Behavior against a modest Pattern and the state is Shatter at τ = 0.36, and when stable (A = 0.12) it is a false lock at τ = 0.10 with Narrative at 0.09. Growth under challenge is a true lock at τ = 0.118 with Adaptation active, and growth raises Pattern and Adaptation together, strictly lowering torsion while preserving Narrative. The growth peak is IVA peak.

  6. The growth peak is IVA peak: τ = 0.10, A = 1.1 (growth_peak_iva_peak).
