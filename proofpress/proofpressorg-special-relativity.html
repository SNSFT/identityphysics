Abstract

Special relativity rests on two postulates: the laws of physics are the same in every inertial frame, and light moves at the same speed c in every frame. Everything else follows from the Lorentz factor γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²) and the boost it defines. This article reduces special relativity to PNBA, with velocity on the Behavior axis, the Lorentz factor on the Adaptation axis, the spacetime interval as the Pattern invariant, ct as Narrative extent and rest mass as Identity Mass. The central identity is γ²(1 − v²/c²) = 1, proved from the definition. From it, the Lorentz boost w′ = γ(w − (v/c)x), x′ = γ(x − (v/c)w) with w = ct preserves w² − x² exactly, so the interval is the same in every frame; the energy-momentum relation E² = (pc)² + (mc²)² follows for E = γmc² and p = γmv; and an event on the light cone stays on it under every boost, which is the second postulate as a theorem. The Lorentz factor is at least 1 and strictly greater for any nonzero velocity, so moving clocks run slow (γΔτ > Δτ) and moving rulers shrink (L₀/γ < L₀). At rest γ = 1, the boost is the identity, and the total energy reduces to mc². Two velocities below c add to a velocity below c. Special relativity is the flat limit of general relativity: with no Behavior there is no transformation and the flat Pattern geometry is left unchanged.
