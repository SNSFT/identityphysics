Abstract

Self-Determination Theory (Deci and Ryan) names three basic psychological needs: competence, relatedness and autonomy. It orders motivation along a continuum from amotivation through external, introjected, identified and integrated regulation to intrinsic motivation. Needs that are met support autonomous motivation and wellbeing; frustrated needs produce controlled motivation and burnout. This article reduces SDT to PNBA. Competence is Pattern, relatedness is Narrative, autonomous action is Behavior, and internalization, the integration of external values into the self, is Adaptation. Reward, punishment and social pressure enter as Fext. The continuum becomes a torsion gradient. External regulation (τ = 0.571) and introjected regulation (0.36) are Shatter phase. Identified (0.129), integrated (0.118) and intrinsic (0.11) are phase locked, and intrinsic motivation adds IVA dominance. Amotivation is Adaptation dropout with Behavior near zero, the same signature as learned helplessness from a different cause. Deci’s overjustification effect appears as Fext raising Behavior and with it torsion.
