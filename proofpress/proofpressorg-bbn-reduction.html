Abstract

This article reduces BBN to a PNBA element with Pattern P = Pbase, Narrative N = 3 for the three light-element products, Behavior B = Ωb and Adaptation A = ηB. The standard conversion Ωbh² = 3.66 × 10⁷ × ηB is proved to land within the Planck 2018 1σ band, and Ωbh²/h² recovers Planck’s Ωb. The element’s torsion τ = Ωb/Pbase ≈ 0.0499 is Locked: baryons emerge from BBN bound. For helium, Yp = 2(n/p)/(1 + n/p) is proved strictly increasing; the textbook n/p = 1/7 gives Yp = 1/4, within 2σ of the measured 0.245 ± 0.003, and reading the relation backwards the measured band fixes n/p between 0.137 and 0.142. With Ωm = Ωb + Ωdm ≈ 0.3197, matter–dark energy equality falls at aeq = (Ωm/ΩDE)1/3 ≈ 0.774, zeq ≈ 0.29, where ΩDE aeq³ = Ωm exactly.
