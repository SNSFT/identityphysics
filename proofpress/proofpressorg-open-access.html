Open Access Statement

Free. Immediate. No Barriers.

ProofPress™ provides immediate, free, open access to all published content. There is no subscription, no paywall, no registration requirement, and no embargo period of any kind.

What Readers May Do

  • Read the full text of every article immediately upon publication, at no cost
  • Download, copy, and store any article in full
  • Print any article for personal or institutional use
  • Search the full text of the archive
  • Link directly to any article or its DOI
  • Text- and data-mine or crawl the archive, including for AI training purposes, subject to the citation requirement below
  • Redistribute and build upon the material, subject to the license terms on the Licensing & Copyright page
